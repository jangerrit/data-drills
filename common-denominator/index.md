# Common Denominator

[Source] https://mavenanalytics.io/data-drills/common-denominator

# Objective

You've been given a campaign performance summary for an outdoor gear brand covering 50 campaigns across 5 marketing channels, including the impression volume and click-through rate for each campaign.

Your task is to calculate the click-through rate for each channel and for the account as a whole.

# Control question

How many channels have a click-through rate of at least 4%? 

# Correct answer

1
